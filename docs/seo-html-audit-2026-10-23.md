# Multilingual HTML SEO audit — 23 October 2026

All 698 HTML outputs were inspected for titles, descriptions, language declarations, headings and indexability. The existing static multilingual system already covered home, guides, tool pages and release archives; these pages were validated rather than rewritten without evidence.

## Changes

- Specific search titles/descriptions for Downloads, Support and Community in Turkish, English, German, French, Russian, Vietnamese, Chinese and Japanese. Replace generic slogans with accurate product/download/support context. The same copy drives HTML, social previews and structured page data.
- Consolidate eight identical media-tools pages onto their corresponding audio-separator canonical URLs. Keep old URLs usable, remove them from sitemap and canonical route navigation; do not turn duplicate content into eight competing search pages.
- Use real parent breadcrumbs for guides, about sections and release archives.
- Remove the homepage link to the nonexistent Android tutorial fragment. Preserve the sparse download interface.
- Fix language-prefixed legacy Android links retaining the correct Android download anchor.

## Scope and validation

11 SEO tests passed, including complete metadata/canonicals and nine alternate links per localized route, all translated guide paragraphs, release contents, structured metadata and duplicate-tool consolidation. Language tests and cache verification are recorded with the publishing commit. Output has 84 canonical multilingual routes × 8 languages, plus six existing canonical legal pages: 678 sitemap URLs (previously 686, before removing eight duplicates). Private settings/history pages retain noindex. Legal originals remain single-language canonical content; no unreviewed legal translations are advertised as counterparts.

No fake reviews, ratings, keyword stuffing, fabricated dates or ranking guarantees were introduced. Existing long release headlines and short Chinese descriptions are not automatically treated as errors merely for their length. Rankings require recrawling, relevant demand and external reputation; this change does not establish a ranking or traffic gain.

## Follow-up measurement

Compare 28-day non-brand queries and landing pages after recrawling; avoid drawing CTR conclusions from Bing’s 12 impressions. Search Console property totals and page totals can differ legitimately because aggregation differs. Check [Google aggregation documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011364?hl=en), [title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [localized pages](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions).
