AI Audio Separator

Separate vocals and music on your device, preview and download. Your file is never uploaded.

Free and open source MIT

Powered by Lilith Audio Engine

ZenithW uses Lilith Audio Engine to separate audio in your browser. Built with TypeScript, the engine runs on your device through WebGPU or WebAssembly; your audio file is not sent to a server.

The engine’s original source code is MIT licensed. You may use, modify and share it, including in commercial projects, while retaining the copyright and license notice. The AI model and other components retain their own license terms.
