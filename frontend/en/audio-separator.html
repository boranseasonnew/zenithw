01

Listen and export

Choose an audio file. Vocals and instrumental are prepared together; listen to either stem or both in sync, then save MP3, FLAC or WAV.

02

Fully local processing

Processing stays on your device. First use downloads about 67 MB of model data and a 27 MB runtime; your audio is not uploaded. A verified model cache can be reused on later runs.

03

Supported files

Supports mono or stereo audio up to 96 MiB and four minutes. For longer recordings, select a section of up to four minutes. Codecs and speed depend on your browser and device; AI output may contain bleed.

OPEN-SOURCE AUDIO ENGINE / LILITH ENGINE

Free and open source MIT

Powered by Lilith Audio Engine

ZenithW uses Lilith Audio Engine to separate audio in your browser. Built with TypeScript, the engine runs on your device through WebGPU or WebAssembly; your audio file is not sent to a server.
