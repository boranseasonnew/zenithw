v7.3 July 2026

youtube quality is now actually quality

In this release

01real 1080p and 4K are actually real now02a sturdier fallback chain

Scroll to explore what changed

Something had been bugging us for a while: why would some YouTube videos land in your hands looking pixelated and 360p-ish, even when you picked 1080p or 4K? We went under the hood and traced every cable until we found the source.

Short version: YouTube protects the real download address of its high-quality video streams behind a hidden signature. Our server did not have the right tools to crack it, so it was quietly falling back to the safest — and lowest — quality available. Not anymore.

real 1080p and 4K are actually real now

We switched our download engine to a method that reaches YouTube's high-quality formats directly, without needing to crack any signature. The result: the quality you pick is now the quality you actually get. In our tests, file sizes jumped into the hundreds of megabytes and the picture came out crystal clear.

a sturdier fallback chain

If the primary method fails for a video, our server now works through a smarter chain of fallbacks — instead of quietly settling for the worst option, it keeps chasing the best quality still available.

This one had been on our list for a long time, and we finally fixed it at the root. No more "I picked 1080p, why does this look blurry."
