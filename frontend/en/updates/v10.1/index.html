v10.1 August 2026

the cat now talks when tapped without shifting the page

In this release

01speech bubble on tap02speech bubble stays inside the viewport

Scroll to explore what changed

We gave the little GitHub cat on the URL box more personality. Tap it for a random line; leave it idle and it may nibble its paw, rarely yawn, or look behind itself.

speech bubble on tap

Tapping the cat shows a random line in the selected language. It briefly gets excited when a fresh link is detected, while small surprise animations appear after ten seconds of idle time.

speech bubble stays inside the viewport

Fixed a bug where long lines could extend beyond the mobile viewport and shift the navbar and chip row horizontally. The bubble is measured before display and moved inward only as much as necessary.

In short: the cat feels more alive while the page stays put. Meow.
