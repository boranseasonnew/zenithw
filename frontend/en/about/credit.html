Who is involved in this work?

Behind ZenithW are people testing early versions, open source projects, and developers patiently fixing minor issues. This page explains concretely the contribution of each.

✳
A BRIEF THANKS

People who pointed out when a link didn't open in the first attempts, a file was saved incorrectly, or the interface got stuck on the phone, guided the project. Sometimes contribution is writing code, sometimes it is showing the problem clearly.

OPEN SOURCE

Tools that make the product possible

Downloading, which appears to be a single process on the interface, is based on years of effort from many projects. The following tools play an important part in ZenithW's development and media processing flow; each has its own community and license.

01 / SOURCE

yt-dlp

Analyzes supported media sources; finds available streams, formats and quality options.

02 / MEDIA

FFmpeg

It combines video and audio streams and executes remux and conversion operations when necessary.

03 / APPLICATION

Python & Flask

It forms the application layer where requests, downloads, and file delivery are managed.

04 / PROGRESS

Socket.IO

It instantly conveys the status and progress information of the transaction to the interface.

Cloudflare is services used for distribution and access, and AWS is services used for processing infrastructure. These companies are not sponsors or partners of ZenithW.
